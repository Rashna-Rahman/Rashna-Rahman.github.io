About me

A CSE student who likes building things that work.

I’m studying Computer Science and Engineering at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB), with a CGPA of 3.80 out of 4.00. I expect to graduate in 2027.

Through individual and group projects I have built web applications, a browser game and desktop systems, working with Java, C, C++, C#, JavaScript and React.js, and with databases including Oracle, SQL and PL/SQL.
